🐢 TamaPoke + Weather

The original TamaPoke for the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.75, plus live weather in the sky, a broom to sweep the poop, and Pokémon that react to the weather by type.

🤖 Made with AI assistance. These add-ons were written with Claude, and parts were ported from botero50's fork. The game itself is Quique Tortosa's TamaPoke. Tested on real hardware, but not reviewed by the original author.

⚠️ Your browser doesn't support Web Serial. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.

1Install or update the firmware

Plug the board in via USB, press the button and pick the port. It takes about a minute.

Browser not supported (use Chrome/Edge). Needs HTTPS.

🛡️ Updating is safe: this installer never erases the board and never writes over the area where your Pokémon and Wi-Fi settings are saved, so they carry over. (The original TamaPoke installer writes one big image that does overwrite it.)

Board not showing up? Use a USB-C cable that carries data, plugged straight into the computer. A brand-new board may need download mode: hold BOOT, tap RESET, let go of BOOT, then try again.

2Connect to the board

Needed for the sprites and the Wi-Fi below. Close the install popup and any other installer tab first (only one program can use the board at a time).

Not connected

3Load the sprites onto the microSD

Only needed once per card. Put a microSD into the board (it formats itself), connect in step 2, then press Load sprites (~40 MB, about 10 minutes). Don't remove the card.

Have your own .bin files?

If it stops partway, press the button again: it picks up where it left off. Restart the board when it's done.


  


  
4Wi-Fi for the real time and weather

  

    
The board joins your Wi-Fi every 30 minutes for a few seconds to set its clock and fetch
       the weather from Open-Meteo, then
       switches the radio off. 2.4 GHz networks only; a separate IoT/guest network is best.

How to play

  
Care for your egg until it hatches, feed it, play, train it and complete them all. Tap the
     creature to pet it; tap the poop to sweep it (the bath still does the full wash); swipe
     to open the Pokédex (sideways), the stat card (up) or set the clock (down). The PWR button turns
     the screen off (short press) or everything off (long press). Water types love the rain, fire
     types love the heat, ice types love snow, electric types love storms.
     More in the repository.
